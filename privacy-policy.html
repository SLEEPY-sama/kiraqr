KiraQR · Your privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how KiraQR handles information when you create, scan, save, or share QR codes using the app.

Effective date: 10 November 2026 Individual developer No account required
Important: QR content is processed on your device by KiraQR. However, KiraQR displays advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process certain device and advertising information as described below. Do not interpret “on-device processing” as meaning that no information is ever shared with a third party.

1. Who operates KiraQR?

KiraQR is an Android application operated by an individual developer. For privacy questions or requests, contact walunjrohit38@gmail.com.

In this policy, “KiraQR”, “the app”, “we”, or “us” refers to the KiraQR application and its developer.

2. Information handled by KiraQR

QR codes and URLs

When you enter a URL or other supported content to create a QR code, KiraQR processes that content locally on your device to generate the QR code. KiraQR does not operate a server that receives or stores the QR content.

QR scanning and camera

KiraQR uses your device camera only to scan QR codes. The camera permission is used to capture the image needed for scanning. Based on the app's current design, scan content is processed on the device and is not uploaded by KiraQR to a KiraQR server.

You can deny camera permission, but QR scanning will not work unless the permission is granted. You can manage camera permission in your Android device settings.

Downloads and sharing

If you choose to download a generated QR image, Android saves it to a location on your device that you select or that the app uses. If you choose to share a QR image or link, Android passes the item to the app or service you select. That receiving app or service handles the information under its own terms and privacy policy.

No in-app QR history

The current version of KiraQR does not provide a QR history feature and does not maintain a KiraQR account or cloud history.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

KiraQR uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob is provided by Google and operates independently from KiraQR.

When ads are requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • IP address and general network information;
  • device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available and permitted;
  • device model, operating system, language, and related technical information;
  • ad impressions, clicks, interactions, and diagnostic information; and
  • information used to deliver, measure, limit, or personalize advertising, depending on your location, consent choices, device settings, and the app's AdMob configuration.

The exact data processed can vary based on Google's SDK version, your device and region, consent status, and the advertising settings used by the app. KiraQR does not control Google's independent data practices.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's guidance on AdMob privacy and consent.

4. Advertising choices and consent

Ad personalization and consent requirements may differ by country or region. Where required, KiraQR should present an appropriate consent or privacy-options flow using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) or another compliant mechanism before requesting ads that require consent.

You may also be able to reset or limit your device's advertising identifier or adjust advertising privacy settings through Android settings. Available controls depend on your Android version and device manufacturer.

5. When information is shared

KiraQR does not sell your QR content. QR content is not sent to a KiraQR-operated server. Information may nevertheless be handled by third parties in these situations:

  • AdMob: Google processes advertising and technical information as described in Section 3.
  • Sharing: when you deliberately share a QR image or URL using another app, that app receives the item you selected.
  • External links: when you open a scanned URL, your browser or the destination website may receive information as part of the normal web request.
  • Legal requirements: information may be disclosed if required by applicable law or valid legal process. The developer does not maintain a KiraQR server containing QR content.

6. Data retention and deletion

KiraQR does not maintain an account or server-side database of QR content. Generated QR content is handled locally. QR images that you download remain on your device until you delete them. Items shared with another app may be retained by that app under its own policies.

Advertising information handled by Google is subject to Google's retention and deletion practices. Refer to Google's privacy resources for details and available controls.

7. Security

We aim to limit KiraQR's handling of QR content to on-device processing. No app, device, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device and Android version updated, and be careful when scanning QR codes from unknown sources.

8. Children's privacy

KiraQR is not designed to request a child's name, contact details, or account information. The app uses a third-party advertising SDK, and advertising treatment may depend on the app's audience settings and applicable law. The developer must ensure the app's target-audience declaration and advertising configuration are appropriate. If you believe information has been provided to the developer in error, contact us.

9. App permissions

  • Camera: used to scan QR codes. It is not intended for recording or uploading video or photos.
  • Storage / media access: if requested by the Android version or implementation, used to save a QR image to your device. Android's system photo picker or MediaStore may be used instead, depending on implementation.
  • Network access: may be used by the advertising SDK to request and display ads. Opening a URL or sharing content also involves the selected external app or service.

Actual permissions can vary by Android version and the app build. Review the permission prompts shown on your device.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when KiraQR's features, SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.
